# Discovery Rounds 01–13 — Consolidated Summary

> **Source:** `Virtual-Music-World-Discovery-Context-and-Requirements.md` (v1.0, 2026-11-15)  
> **Rule:** Never silently rewrite historical discovery decisions. Track changes via Decision Log.

| Round | Topic | Key Confirmed Decisions | Open / Clarify |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Product Vision & Core Concept | Combines 3D game, music platform, social world, live performance. All music genres. Spontaneous & scheduled events. Admin room oversight. | Reconcile Round 01 Admin room management vs Round 06 all-user room creation (`TBD-01`). |
| **02** | Auditorium & 3D World | Central Lobby + independent Auditoriums. **Small Room first** (10–20 people) for MVP. Hybrid camera. Restricted aisle/seat movement. 2D seat map + manual movement. Warm, cozy atmosphere. | Exact capacity (performers included?), hold duration, MVP seat tiers (`TBD-04`, `TBD-05`). |
| **03** | Character & Animation | Anime Chibi presets (no full creator, no facial expressions). Basic animations (Idle, Walk, Sit, Stand, Clap, Cheer, Wave, Dance, AFK). Auto-clap at song end. Rhythm reactions. Performer outfit/instrument customization. | Rhythm trigger (`TBD-07`), max visible avatars (`TBD-06`), instrument animation mapping. |
| **04** | Live Music & Instrument Input | Physical instruments, MIDI, DAW, prerecorded audio. Piano, guitar/bass, violin/cello, drums/pads. Mic, interface, MIDI, virtual audio. Solo/Duo/Band/Orchestra. Live↔prerecorded switching. Sound check & invite performers. USB/USB-C where compatible. | Performer voice comm (`TBD-08`), browser/OS/Companion compatibility (`TBD-09`), disconnect recovery (`TBD-11`). |
| **05** | Multiplayer & Social | Small Room 10–20. Collapsible livestream-style text chat. **No voice chat in Auditorium.** Invite / room code. No friend list/party/follow in MVP. Stronger interaction between songs. | Non-financial gift presentation in MVP (`TBD-16`), chat rate limits, block/mute scope. |
| **06** | Stage, Event & Performance | All users can create rooms within limits. Roles: Admin, Host, Artist/Performer. Fixed stage initially. Per-song lighting scripts. Editable prepared setlist. Recording/replay is Future (Admin-managed). | Account room limits (`TBD-03`), Host permissions (`TBD-12`), Artist verification (`TBD-13`), lighting authority (`TBD-14`). |
| **07** | Economy & Monetization | **No currency, no paid tickets, no artist monetization, no premium accounts, no income/expense system in MVP.** Future store: outfits, characters, instruments, effects, decorations. | Future monetization only; MVP must not depend on payment infra. |
| **08** | UI/UX & Visual Direction | Landing → Explore (list/calendar/search/featured) → Concert Detail → 2D Seat Selection → 3D Auditorium. Minimal Lobby & Auditorium UI. Collapsible chat. Professional music-platform aesthetic. | Validate exact screens/routes during UX spec. |
| **09** | Technical Architecture | Browser-first. React + TS + Three.js / R3F (`CONFIRMED`). Optional Desktop Companion App. Modular Monolith backend (`CONFIRMED`). Grouped multiplayer sync. 100–500 CCU. Audio continuity & sync critical. | Realtime transport, audio delivery, DB/cache, cloud, observability, numeric thresholds (`TBD-10`, `TBD-22`). |
| **10** | AI-First Development | Maximize AI automation. Agent Manager + specialist agents. **Obsidian as central knowledge base.** AI asset pipeline & AI-assisted QA. Clarify requirements before decomposing tasks. Human approval gates. | Operationalized via `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/skills/`, and `07-AI-Agents/`. |
| **11** | Security, Privacy & Legal | Email/Password + Social Login. AI + Admin + community moderation (Mute, Kick, Temp/Perm Ban). Music usage rules, licensing, copyright complaints. No recording/replay in MVP. **Target age 16+.** Protect Audience & Performer. | Session security, report SLA, age verification (`TBD-18`), copyright workflow (`TBD-19`), AI moderation bounds (`TBD-20`). |
| **12** | Business Model & Roadmap | Target all user groups. **Vietnam-first.** **Public Launch** after readiness gates. Complete end-to-end MVP. Low latency & sync as differentiator. No community-growth strategy needed yet. | Release metrics & thresholds (`TBD-21`). |
| **13** | Strategic Prioritization | Must-have / Should-have / Future. Small Room first. All confirmed inputs & device categories (with capability tiers). Audio, FPS, multiplayer, security are launch gates. **North Star: Audience Retention.** | Resolve device capability tiers & scope conflict (`TBD-09`, `TBD-23`, `TBD-24`). |
