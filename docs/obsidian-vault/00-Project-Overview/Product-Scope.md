# Product Scope

## Prioritization Model (CONFIRMED)
Requirements are divided into **Must-have**, **Should-have**, and **Future**. Only Must-have items are mandatory for Public Launch.

## Must-have — Proposed Launch Baseline (`PROPOSED` classification; needs PO confirmation)
- Email/Password + Social Login (`SEC-01`, `SEC-02`)
- Landing page & concert discovery (`UX-01`, `UX-05`)
- Minimal Lobby (`UX-02`) & Small Room Auditorium 10–20 capacity (`AR-01`, `AR-02`)
- 2D seat selection before entering 3D room + restricted aisle/seat movement (`AR-06`, `AR-07`, `UX-06`)
- Chibi preset avatars & basic animations (`CR-01`, `CR-02`, `CR-04`, `CR-05`, `CR-06`)
- Room presence & grouped multiplayer sync (`TA-04`)
- Performer setup, sound check, setlist, lighting cues, live/prerecorded switching (`LMR-06`, `LMR-08`, `SM-07`, `SM-08`)
- Agreed instrument/audio input integrations (`LMR-01`..`LMR-03`, `LMR-05`)
- Collapsible livestream-style text chat & basic reactions (`MR-02`, `UX-04`)
- Invite & room-code joining (`MR-05`)
- Host/Admin moderation, reporting, 16+ age policy, privacy controls, copyright workflow (`SEC-04`..`SEC-18`)

## Should-have (`PROPOSED`)
- VIP Hall & Private Room (`AR-03`, `AR-04`)
- Advanced seat reservation, richer discovery/calendar, more outfits, advanced rhythm reactions, expanded Admin analytics, more stage templates.

## Future (`CONFIRMED` direction — DO NOT BUILD IN MVP)
- Recording & Replay (`SM-09`, `SEC-12`, `SEC-13`)
- Virtual currency, store, paid tickets, monetized gifts, artist payouts, premium accounts (`EC-01`..`EC-08`)
- Stage customization (`SM-06`)
- Full social graph: friends, parties, follows (`MR-08`)
- International expansion & large-scale venues.

> PRD & PoC work must define exact device capability tiers and what "support" means per workflow without silently reducing requirements.
