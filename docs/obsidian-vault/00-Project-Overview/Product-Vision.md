# Product Vision

## Vision Statement
> Build a Virtual Music World where people can perform, discover, and experience live music together through expressive 3D concert spaces, real instruments, synchronized avatars, and reliable low-latency audio.

## Core Concept
Virtual Music World is an online 3D multiplayer music and concert platform combining:
- A 3D multiplayer game experience (Anime Chibi avatars in warm, intimate auditoriums).
- A virtual music platform.
- A social world / metaverse-style concert environment.
- A live music performance platform (real instruments, mics, interfaces, MIDI, DAWs, prerecorded audio).

## Core Value Proposition
**Low-latency music performance with reliable synchronization.**

The product is judged primarily by the quality and reliability of the live concert experience—not merely visual complexity or feature count.

## Product Principles (CONFIRMED)
1. **Concert first:** Prioritize performance and audience experience over unrelated social features.
2. **Real music input:** Support real instruments and professional/semi-professional audio workflows.
3. **Low latency and synchronization:** Audio continuity, latency, and audio-animation sync are critical.
4. **Immersive but focused 3D:** Realistic venue feel without unnecessary free-roaming complexity.
5. **End-to-end completeness:** Complete concert journey in MVP, even if secondary features are simplified.
6. **Controlled scope:** Strict split into Must-have, Should-have, and Future.
7. **AI-first development:** Automate analysis, decomposition, implementation, asset pipelines, and QA with human approval gates.
8. **Safety and trust:** Protect both audience members and performers (16+ target age).
9. **No early monetization pressure:** Focus on product quality before payments or revenue systems.
